# Official-source verification log

Checked on **9 November 2026 (India)**. The JSON records are curated paraphrases,
with practical suggestions explicitly distinguished from statutory requirements.
Hindi/Hinglish versions are app translations, not official legal translations.
No judicial decisions or case-outcome predictions are included.

  verified its links to the Act, 2020 rules, corrigendum, 2021 amendment, and 2026 amendment.

## Applicability and review

Records provide general information, never a determination that a provision
applies to the user's incident. Facts are user allegations; evidence has not
been inspected. A useful documentation list and professional-help recommendation
are app preparation/safety suggestions, not claims of legal compulsion.

All entries require rechecking after **31 December 2026**. Retrieval excludes
entries outside their checked/review interval, rather than continuing with an
unreviewed knowledge set after amendment commencement. Revisit the official
library, inspect any changes and effective dates, review all three languages,
update the JSON and this log, and rerun the offline tests before extending the

is claimed. Specific deadlines, mandatory notices, automatic compensation, criminal
liability, and judgments are outside this small knowledge base.
